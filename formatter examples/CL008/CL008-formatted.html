If you need help, the following loss-mitigation options may be possible (most are subject to lender approval).
• Repayment Plan – A Repayment Plan allows for gradual repayment of the past due amount while your regularly scheduled contractual monthly payments are still being made.
• Forbearance – A payment Forbearance temporarily gives you more time to pay your contractual monthly payment.
• Standalone Partial Claim – A Standalone Partial Claim allows for a reinstatement of the mortgage loan with a Partial Claim for Borrowers able to resume their contractual mortgage loan payments.
• Standalone Loan-Modification – A Standalone Loan-Modification allows the mortgage term to be extended by 360 or 480-months (30 or 40-years). The Standalone Loan-Modification would also allow for a minimum 25% reduction of the monthly Principal and Interest (P&I) portion of the mortgage loan.
• Combination Loan-Modification & Partial Claim – A Combination Loan-Modification & Partial Claim allows the mortgage term to be extended by 360 or 480-months (30 or 40-years) and includes a Partial Claim when funds are available. The Combination Loan-Modification & Partial Claim allows for a minimum 25% reduction of the month Principal & Interest (P&I) portion of the mortgage loan.
• Payment Supplement – A Payment Supplement utilizes partial claim funds to bring the loan current along with the subsequent provision of a Monthly Principal Reduction (MoPR) applied to the principal due each month for a period of 36-months (3-years), providing payment relief without modifying the mortgage loan.
• Outside of the Waterfall Loan-Modification (OWL) – An OWL provides a permanent change to one or more of the terms on the mortgage loan to achieve a minimum of $1 reduction to the Principal & Interest (P&I) portion of the mortgage loan, when the Borrower(s) have been unresponsive to Commerce Bank during the delinquency.
• Pre-Foreclosure Sale – If unable to continue making the contractual monthly payment, the best option may be to find more affordable housing. As an alternative to Foreclosure, you may be able to sell your home and use the proceeds to pay down or pay off your current loan.
• Deed-in-Lieu of Foreclosure – An FHA Deed-in-Lieu of Foreclosure allows for the transfer of ownership in the property to FHA and/or Commerce Bank.

For help exploring your options, the Federal government provides contact information which can be accessed by contacting the Department of Housing and Urban Development at: http://www.hud.gov/offices/hsg/sfh/hcc/hcs.cfm or by calling 800-569-4287.
