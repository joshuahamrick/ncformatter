While you are on active duty, {[plsMatrix.CompanyShortName]} Home Loans is committed to supporting you in any way possible. As an active-duty servicemember, you may be eligible for certain mortgage-related protections under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA).

These protections may include, but are not limited to, the following:
• Interest Rate Reduction: The interest rate on mortgage debt incurred by a servicemember, or jointly by a servicemember and spouse, prior to entering military service may be reduced to 6% during the period of active military service and for one year thereafter.
• Foreclosure Protections: A court may delay or stop a foreclosure action that is initiated during active military service or within one year following the end of service. Unless a creditor obtains a valid court order, any sale, foreclosure, or seizure of property occurring during this protected period may be deemed invalid.
• Waiver of Late Charges: Late charges that become due after the servicemember is called to active duty must be waived.
In addition, special forbearance or other loss mitigation options may be available if you are experiencing financial hardship.

How to Request SCRA Benefits
To request SCRA protections, please submit a written request along with one or more of the following documents:
• Military orders showing the active-duty start date and military branch;
• A letter on official military letterhead from an executive or commanding officer that includes the active-duty start date and military branch; or
• Any other relevant military documentation.
