Enclosed please find the HUD-90035 – Pre-foreclosure Sale Procedure Information Sheet issued by the
U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD).

This document provides important information regarding HUD's Pre-Foreclosure Sale (PFS) Program, which may allow eligible homeowners to sell their property for fair market value—even if that amount is less than the total mortgage balance—in order to avoid foreclosure.

Under this program:

• You may be permitted to sell your home and satisfy your mortgage obligation.
• Eligible owner-occupants may qualify for up to $3,000 in relocation assistance.
• A successful PFS may have less impact on your credit than a completed foreclosure.

The enclosed form explains the program requirements, eligibility considerations, and next steps.
Participation is voluntary, and you are under no obligation to proceed.
